# 07 — Both of the bar's refusals balk, and say why

**What to build:** The bar turns two things down, and today each does half the job: a source the
backend will not set puts its reason above the input and the bar sits still, where a press the bar
will not take swings it and says not a word. After this both do both — the bar
[balks](../../../CONTEXT.md#balk) and says why. Motion is the channel that says a refusal happened
*now*; words are the channel that says what was wrong; neither answers the other's question.

What that buys is the **repeat**. Submit the same unsettable source twice and the sentence is
identical both times, so nothing on the page tells a second refusal from a bar that has stopped
listening.

The refused press gains `Provide a label or abort with Esc` — the bar's own sentence, one for every
`required` naming, so it goes on reading that and nothing else about the mark and a kind that becomes
drawable later is covered untouched. Naming Escape bends nothing at rest: the line is empty until
something is refused, and a refusal that is a dead end is no answer at all.

Both refusals share the one line, and it stands for the last of them — the rule the export's region
already states for itself. So pressing away from a source that will not set replaces why it will not
set; one Enter re-asks.

Nothing else in the editor gains motion. A refused [plop](../../../CONTEXT.md#plop) and a write the
filesystem would not make each end the thing that caused them, so neither has a repeat to tell apart,
and the labels a diagram read from a file could not set are no
[refusal](../../../CONTEXT.md#refusal) at all — nothing was attempted.
