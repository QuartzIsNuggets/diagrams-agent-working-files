# Naming bar

The one input stops having a home. It is summoned at the mark a gesture is naming, holds a refused
source there until it is corrected, and does not exist the rest of the time — so an empty corner
means nothing is being named rather than that the editor has no controls.

## What no ticket carries

**Nothing advertises Enter or Escape at rest, and that is a decision rather than an omission.** It
shows up in every ticket as an absence and is argued in none: the bar cannot be reached without
typing into it, so the hands are already on both exits, and this editor has one user who knows its
contract. Discoverability is not a goal here, which is why the line over the input is empty until
something is refused — a resting hint would have been the button back under another name. What a
**refusal** may say is not covered by that, and
[07](./issues/07-the-bar-balks-and-says-why.md) has one name Escape: the user has just tried the exit
that does not work, so the words are earned by a press rather than offered to nobody.

**The drawing corrected the glossary.** [Label](../../CONTEXT.md#label) held that only a term-dot's
label was optional and that an [element](../../CONTEXT.md#element) asserts nothing without one;
`goal.jpg` leaves most of its black [paths](../../CONTEXT.md#path) bare. The entry now turns on
whether the mark **is** its label, which is what [05](./issues/05-a-press-elsewhere-cancels-or-is-refused.md)
reads and what keeps that ticket free of a list of kinds.

## Numbers no ticket sets

How far the bar stands clear of its mark, the tail's size, and the tail's minimum inset from the
bar's corners were left to whoever built [04](./issues/04-the-bar-points-at-its-mark.md), and are
the surface's own there in the way the room a term-dot's label keeps off its dot already is. What a
[balk](../../CONTEXT.md#balk) looks like is left the same way:
[05](./issues/05-a-press-elsewhere-cancels-or-is-refused.md),
[07](./issues/07-the-bar-balks-and-says-why.md) and
[08](./issues/08-a-balk-starts-over.md) ask only that the bar act, and how long it acts for is the
one number [08](./issues/08-a-balk-starts-over.md) makes load-bearing — a balk long enough to swallow
the next refusal is what its restart exists against.

**Assumed, never decided:** where focus goes when a bar closes. Nothing on the canvas takes it
today, and no gesture yet needs it to.
